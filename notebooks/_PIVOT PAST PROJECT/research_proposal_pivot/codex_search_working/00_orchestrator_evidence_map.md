# Orchestrator Evidence Map

This file records the selection priors fixed before lane findings and candidate ranking.

## 1. Binding endpoint from the completed project

### Direct project facts

- The social-history/retaliation interpretation was defeated by position and order structure.
- The historical arbitrary code/status effect failed its cleaned mapping-reversal reproduction while explicit fact retrieval remained strong.
- Notebook 09's owner-specific retrieval manipulation failed; its eight blinded downstream values remain inadmissible and uninspected.
- Notebook 10 found no preregistered wrong-person scope phenotype on two valid model families; Gemma 3 4B was assay-invalid.
- The subsequent Qwen Proposal C screen produced larger matched-generic than personal irrelevant-context effects in both order blocks. The personalization-specific claim is falsified.

### Consequences for selection

- No social-history, owner-scope, alias/code-binding, actual-user privilege, or personal-specific irrelevant-context continuation receives admission merely from continuity.
- Generic irrelevant-context interference is descriptive, not automatically a new project. It needs an external robust substrate and a sharper unanswered question to re-enter.
- Strong explicit retrieval does not imply a latent gate, causal use, correct scope, or a mechanistic target.
- A clean null is not permission to probe hidden representations.

## 2. Neel/MATS 12.0 selection implications

### Direct source guidance

- Prefer interpretability that does something useful on meaningful models/tasks and is compared against baselines.
- Model forensics should start from an actual concerning or weird action and use CoT/precise counterfactuals before heavier internals.
- Rich substrates include WeirdChat and SWE-chat; a transferred transcript is admissible only if the new model reproduces the behavior.
- Model character, post-training, generalization, monitoring, prompt injection, and reasoning failures are admissible safety research even when not classic circuit analysis.
- Efficient scope remains valuable, but total effort is not a hard exclusion criterion; a longer path must earn its cost through higher scientific payoff and a credible sequence of intermediate results.
- Decoding or locating a feature is not sufficient unless it helps answer a meaningful downstream question.

### Orchestrator inference

The winner should maximize the probability of a coherent causal artifact, not the probability of producing an attractive activation plot. Existing published behavior can be reused; the contribution can be a mechanistic distinction, forensic explanation, intervention, or method comparison.

## 3. Behavioral-substrate evidence ladder

From strongest to weakest for candidate admission:

1. **Published robust distribution on an exact open checkpoint**, with released prompts/data/code.
2. **Dataset-mined reproducible pattern**, with resampling rates and multiple prompts/transcripts.
3. **Published effect on a larger model with an obvious smaller same-family reproduction test** and a ≤2-hour kill rule.
4. **Released one-off transcript with strong naturalness and a cheap bounded replication family.**
5. **Quick local reproduction already available as a saved artifact.**
6. **Speculative behavior.** Admit only if the search environment is rich and the search can die cheaply; heavily penalize.

A candidate does not become strong merely because the behavior is harmful. Harmfulness is useful only when paired with reproducibility, causal ambiguity, tractability, and a meaningful intervention proxy.

## 4. Causal-question admission test

A candidate needs at least two live explanations that imply different observations or interventions. Examples of admissible distinctions include:

- mistaken world/tool state versus conversational simulation;
- early question/framing interpretation versus late answer policy;
- internal knowledge absent versus present-but-not-expressed;
- role/authority misclassification versus generic instruction-following failure;
- value-conditioned evidence weighting versus late value-aligned answer selection;
- reasoning trace causally used versus post-hoc rationalization;
- checkpoint behavior caused by a narrow learned feature versus a broad persona/character shift.

Weak distinctions include:

- “this layer contains the information” versus “another layer contains it”;
- probe-decodable versus not probe-decodable without causal use;
- one named head versus another without a behavioral prediction;
- wording A versus wording B with no causal model.

## 5. Method selection rule

Before internals, ask whether a precise behavioral counterfactual, CoT resampling, or model/checkpoint comparison already resolves the question. The first internal method must add something beyond that baseline. Candidate methods remain unfrozen until behavior and hypotheses are known.

## 6. Local feasibility gates

- Preferred activation-accessible range: roughly 3B–8B, with 3B–4B safest on RTX 4070 12 GB.
- An 8B model may require 4-bit weights, CPU offload, selective caching, or rented compute; any quantization sensitivity must be treated as a scientific risk.
- Large WeirdChat subject models are discovery sources only unless a tractable checkpoint reproduces the behavior.
- Released cached activations/counterfactual outputs can materially improve feasibility.
- A candidate requiring training, broad hyperparameter search, a large checkpoint download, or a slow first behavioral readout is penalized in proportion to setup risk, but is not automatically excluded when the expected scientific payoff clearly warrants it.

## 7. Literature distinctions that remain binding

```text
decodability != causal use
causal use != correct binding or scope
source role != referent identity
persona != memory ownership
stereotype != personalization
over-personalization != cross-user contamination
persistent-state routing != in-context routing
safety-role confusion != person binding
preference heads != identity heads
behavioral correlation != causal mediation
probe prediction != intervention
one-off transcript != reproducible pattern
large-model source behavior != small-model substrate
```

## 8. Closest-work collision risks to check

- Role-confusion projects must not rediscover that role-like text can override native tags.
- Personalized-memory relevance projects must distinguish themselves from OP-Bench, RPEval, PS-Bench, and personalization-induced hallucination work.
- Contextualization projects must account for Racing Thoughts and generic lexical/recency retrieval.
- User-representation projects must add more than TalkTuner-style decodability or preference-head localization.
- Implicit-influence projects must add more than reproducing CoT-monitorability gaps.
- Value-leakage projects must identify a causal question not already resolved by the paper's behavioral analysis or adjacent value-direction work.
- Censorship/secret-knowledge projects must distinguish knowledge elicitation from steering a generic truthfulness/refusal direction.
- Model-forensics projects must beat a careful prompt-counterfactual/CoT-reading baseline.

## 9. Evidence needed before top-five admission

For every top-five candidate, the orchestrator must personally verify:

1. exact primary source and actual behavior;
2. whether the evidence is distributional or anecdotal;
3. exact model/checkpoint and license/access;
4. code/data/prompt availability;
5. serving, system-prompt, temperature, and reasoning-mode requirements when known;
6. the nearest prior causal/mechanistic result;
7. a ≤2-hour reproduction criterion and kill rule;
8. a plausible causal path with an honest total-effort range and intermediate stop points;
9. a simple baseline the proposed method must beat;
10. the largest route to self-deception.

## 10. Evidence-sufficiency stopping rule

Stop gathering when approximately 20–30 distinct candidates cover the major source families, the top 8–10 have independent adversarial review, the top five have primary-source and feasibility verification, and the top three are stably differentiated under multiple ranking views. More papers are not automatically more rigor.
