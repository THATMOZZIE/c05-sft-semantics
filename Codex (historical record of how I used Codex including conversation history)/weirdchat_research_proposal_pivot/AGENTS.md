## Selection philosophy

Prefer:

```text
real behavior
→ quick reproduction
→ competing explanations
→ targeted falsification
→ causal question
→ minimal intervention
```

Avoid another speculative synthetic factorial as the opening move.

A known published/released behavior is a valid starting substrate. Novelty can come from:
- causal explanation;
- mechanistic distinction;
- intervention;
- generalization;
- forensic diagnosis;
- monitoring improvement;
- method evaluation.
